Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

REVID, Inc. ("REVID," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are based in Wyoming, United States.

What we collect

  • Vehicle data you choose to read. When you connect an OBD-II adapter, REVID may read diagnostic codes and live engine readings (such as RPM, coolant temperature, and fuel trim) that you request. This data is processed to provide your diagnosis and is associated with your session.
  • Account information. If you create an account, we collect your email address and basic profile information you provide.
  • Usage information. We collect limited analytics about how the service is used, such as which features are accessed, to improve the product.

How we use your data

  • To provide diagnostic guidance, cost estimates, and vehicle comparison features.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve REVID.
  • To communicate with you about your account or the service.

We never sell your data

We do not sell your personal or vehicle data to third parties. We do not rent or trade it for marketing purposes.

Data sharing

We share information only as needed to operate the service — for example, with service providers who help us host, secure, or deliver REVID under written agreements — or when required by law.

Data retention

We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the service and as described here, or as required by law. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Your choices

  • You can choose not to connect an adapter or read specific vehicle data.
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting support@revidinc.com.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

Children

REVID is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@revidinc.com or write to REVID, Inc., Wyoming, U.S.
