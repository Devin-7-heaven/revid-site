About

We're putting the diagnostic in the driver's hands.

REVID · Wyoming, U.S.

REVID was built around a simple frustration: a check-engine light shouldn't cost a hundred-plus dollars and a trip across town just to learn what it means. The data is already sitting in your vehicle — the OBD-II port has been standard on U.S. passenger cars since 1996. We just hadn't been given a good way to read it and understand it.

What we're building

REVID is a diagnostic companion that reads your vehicle's live data and stored codes and turns them into plain-English guidance: a likely cause, a fix path, a cost estimate, and a clear call on whether the job is a safe DIY or one to hand to a pro. It runs in a browser, pairs with an inexpensive Bluetooth adapter, and is built around the everyday driver — not the shop.

Where we are

REVID is in early access. We're refining the experience with real drivers, adding to our code library, and improving the guidance engine. There's no paid plan yet — early users can try it free during launch.

What we believe

  • Drivers deserve to understand their own vehicles without paying for a guess.
  • Guidance should be matched to your skill — not push you into repairs you can't safely do.
  • Your data is yours. We never sell it.
