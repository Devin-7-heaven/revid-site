An OBD-II vehicle

Designed for most U.S. passenger vehicles sold 1996 and newer, which use the standard OBD-II port.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What vehicles does REVID work with?

REVID is designed for most U.S. passenger vehicles from 1996 and newer, which use the standard OBD-II port. Heavy-duty diesel and some hybrid/electric powertrains may report differently; compatibility varies by vehicle and adapter.

Which OBD-II dongle should I get?

Any inexpensive ELM327 Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) adapter works. Expect to spend roughly $25–45. Avoid Wi-Fi-only or cheap no-name adapters that don't follow the BLE standard — pairing and live data can be unreliable.

Does it work on iPhone?

REVID uses direct browser Bluetooth, which Chrome and Edge on Android, Windows, and macOS support. iOS browsers don't currently support this connection, so use an Android device or a laptop instead.

Do you sell my data?

No. We don't sell your personal or vehicle data. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

How accurate is the diagnosis?

REVID turns your live data and codes into a likely cause and fix path — it's guidance, not a guarantee. Always confirm with a professional before spending on parts, especially for safety-critical systems.

When should I just call a mechanic?

For brakes, steering, airbags, fuel systems, or any repair you're not confident about — call a qualified mechanic. REVID helps you know when a job is beyond your comfort level.
